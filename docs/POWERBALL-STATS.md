## Chi-square goodness of fit vs uniform

| Pool | chi-square | df | p-value (approx.) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| White balls (7175 ball draws over 69 numbers) | 75.27 | 68 | 0.255 |
| Powerball (1435 draws over 26 numbers) | 24.64 | 25 | 0.483 |

p-value = P(chi-square >= observed) under a fair, uniform draw, computed with the regularized upper incomplete gamma function Q(df/2, x/2). Note: the 5 whites in a draw are sampled without replacement, so the white-ball test is a close approximation, not exact.

**Plain-language conclusion:** the white balls give p = 0.25, far above 0.05, so there is no evidence of bias; the Powerballs give p = 0.48, far above 0.05, so there is no evidence of bias. The spread between "hot" and "cold" numbers is about what pure chance produces with 1435 draws (roughly 104 hits per white ball and 55 per Powerball). Nothing here suggests the draws are anything but uniform random.
