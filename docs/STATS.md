## Chi-square goodness of fit vs uniform

| Pool | chi-square | df | p-value (approx.) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| White balls (845 ball draws over 70 numbers) | 69.31 | 69 | 0.467 |
| Mega Ball (169 draws over 24 numbers) | 22.29 | 23 | 0.503 |

p-value = P(chi-square >= observed) under a fair, uniform draw, computed with the regularized upper incomplete gamma function Q(df/2, x/2). Note: the 5 whites in a draw are sampled without replacement, so the white-ball test is a close approximation, not exact.

**Plain-language conclusion:** the white balls give p = 0.47, far above 0.05, so there is no evidence of bias; the Mega Balls give p = 0.50, far above 0.05, so there is no evidence of bias. The spread between "hot" and "cold" numbers is about what pure chance produces with only 169 draws (roughly 12 hits per white ball and 7 per Mega Ball). Nothing here suggests the draws are anything but uniform random.
